Imperial College London · Group project · June 2025

Validating the Higgs Boson

A statistical analysis of simulated LHC collision data.

Project summaryHTMLPDF ↓

Overview

In a group of four, I worked on a simulated collider-data analysis designed to test whether a Higgs-like signal was supported by the data.

Method

We generated and analysed collision data using NumPy and Matplotlib. The fitting workflow used maximum-likelihood estimation and \(\chi^2\) minimisation to compare signal-and-background models.

Outcome

The reduced \(\chi^2\) improved from \(2.83\) to \(1.13\) after including the Higgs contribution at the simulated mass. The analysis was documented in a formal group report; this public page and its PDF are a concise project summary, not the original report.
